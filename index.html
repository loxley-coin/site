LOXLEY

A Fair & Open Presale of the Coin call'd LOX,
upon Robinhood Chain, at one fixt price for shepherd & sheriff alike.

Whereas one billion LOX have been struck under the greenwood tree, it is hereby made known that four hundred million of them are offer'd for sale at this fair, at the rate of one Ether for two hundred million LOX — neither more nor less, no haggling, no sliding scales, the same reckoning for every purse that comes to the counter.

Let no man say he was not told. What is paid is chalk'd upon the stall ledger in the open, and when the fair is done & claims are open'd, each man collects the coin that bears his name.

How a Man May Take Part

  1. Weigh in. While the fair is open, lay ETH on the counter. The stall chalks up thy LOX at the fixt rate, there and then.
  2. The bell closes the fair. When time runs out — or the 2 ETH stall limit is reach'd — no more coin is sold.
  3. Collect thy coin. Claims open right after the close. Come back to this stall, press “Collect My LOX”, and the coin moves to thy purse.
